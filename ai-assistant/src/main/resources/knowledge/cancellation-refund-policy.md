# Cancellation & refund policy

## Can I cancel a booking?

You can cancel a booking while it is awaiting payment (PENDING_PAYMENT). The seats are released straight away and
nothing is charged.

## Can I cancel or refund a confirmed ticket?

No. Confirmed tickets cannot be cancelled or refunded. Once a booking is CONFIRMED the sale is final.

## Failed payments

If a payment is declined you are not charged. You can try again with a new payment attempt as long as your hold
lasts (10 minutes from booking). After the hold ends the booking expires and you would need to book again.

## Payment after the hold expired

If your payment goes through after the hold already expired (for example a slow bank response), the booking can
no longer be confirmed. The money is refunded to you automatically; you don't need to ask for it.

## Payment for a cancelled booking

If you cancel a booking and a payment for it still completes afterwards, that payment is refunded automatically.

## How long does a refund take?

Automatic refunds are requested immediately. If the payment provider is temporarily unavailable we retry until the
refund succeeds.
