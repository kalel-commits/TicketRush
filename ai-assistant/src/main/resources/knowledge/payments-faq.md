# Payments FAQ

## Was I charged twice?

No. Each booking can have only one payment in progress or completed at a time; a second attempt for the same
booking is refused before any money moves. Retrying the same payment request returns the same result instead of
charging again.

## My payment says "processing"

Sometimes the payment provider answers slowly. Your payment then shows as processing while we confirm the result
with the provider; this usually settles within a minute. Do not pay again: if the charge went through, your booking
is confirmed; if it did not, the attempt fails and you can retry.

## My payment was declined

A declined payment is not charged. You can retry with a new attempt while your seats are still held (10 minutes
from booking).

## The payment service is unavailable

If our payment provider is down, your attempt fails immediately and nothing is charged. Try again in a few minutes
while your hold lasts.
