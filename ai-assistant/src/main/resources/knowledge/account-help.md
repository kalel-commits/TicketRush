# Account help

## How do I create an account?

Register with your email address and a password. Then log in to book seats.

## I can't log in

Check the email address and password. For security we don't say which one was wrong.

## Who can see my bookings?

Only you. Bookings and payments are visible to the account that made them.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@ticketrush.example with your booking id (shown on the booking page). Support replies within one
business day.
