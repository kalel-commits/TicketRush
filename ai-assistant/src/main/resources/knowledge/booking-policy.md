# Booking policy

## How many seats can I book?

You can book at most 4 seats per event. The limit counts every booking for that event that is being held,
awaiting payment or confirmed. Cancelled, expired and failed bookings do not count, so their seats are freed for
you to book again. A request that would take you above 4 seats is refused with "seat limit exceeded".

## How long is a seat held?

When you book, your seats are held for you for 10 minutes. Pay within those 10 minutes to confirm the booking.
If you don't, the hold expires: the booking becomes EXPIRED and the seats go back on sale for everyone.

## When does the sale open?

Each event has a sale opening time. Before it, booking attempts are refused with "ticket sales for this event are
not open". When the sale opens many people book at the same moment; if the seat you picked was taken a moment
earlier you get "seats unavailable" and can pick another seat.

## Booking statuses explained

- HOLDING: we are reserving your seats right now (a second or two).
- PENDING_PAYMENT: your seats are held for you; pay before the hold ends (10 minutes).
- CONFIRMED: you have paid and the seats are yours.
- EXPIRED: the 10-minute hold ended without a successful payment; the seats were released.
- CANCELLED: you cancelled the booking before paying; the seats were released.
- FAILED: the booking could not be completed (for example the seats were taken or our systems were busy). You
  were not charged.

## Why did I get "seats unavailable"?

Someone else held or bought that seat first. Refresh the seat map and choose another available seat.
