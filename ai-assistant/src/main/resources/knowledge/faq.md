# TicketRush FAQ

## What is TicketRush?

TicketRush sells tickets for events with assigned seats. Pick your seats on the seat map, book them, and pay within
10 minutes to confirm.

## How do I find events?

Browse the events list. Each event shows its venue, date, price and how many seats are still available. The
available-seat number can lag by a few seconds during a busy sale; the seat map is always current.

## Why was my request refused with "too many requests"?

To keep sales fair we limit how fast one account can send booking and payment requests. Wait a moment and try again.

## Can the assistant book or cancel for me?

No. The assistant only answers questions and can look up events and your own bookings. To book, pay or cancel, use
the buttons in the app; the assistant can link you to the right page.
